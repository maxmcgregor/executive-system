# Onboarding

- [ ] Setup
- [ ] Vision
- [ ] Values & Principles
- [ ] Profile
- [ ] Board Members
- [ ] Current Work & Goals
- [ ] First Board Meeting

Claude works through these with you, one conversation at a time. Unchecked items are what put the
system in onboarding mode -- once they're all checked, `/start` switches to normal daily use. You can
stop after any step and pick it up next session.

**What "done" looks like:** your vision, values, and profile written in your own words; a board of
perspectives that will actually push back; `state/projects.md` listing everything you have in flight
with a next action on each; 2-3 controllable goals for the quarter; and one board meeting on the
record. After that, `/start` has something real to read every morning.
